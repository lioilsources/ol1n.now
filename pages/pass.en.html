Questions

Do I have to sign in anywhere?

Not on iPhone or iPad. The apps hand the Pass over through Apple's shared keychain, so being signed in to your Apple account as usual is enough. On Android the installed apps hand the Pass to each other; if you have only one, you can link it with your Google account or with an eight-character code shown by the app that has the Pass.

I bought the Pass on iPhone, does it work on Android?

It does. Show a code in the app that has the Pass and enter it in the app on the other platform. The code is valid for 15 minutes and works once.

What if I delete an app or change phones?

Use “Restore purchases” in any of my apps. The purchase is tied to your App Store or Google Play account, not to the device.

Does Family Sharing work?

On the App Store yes, the Pass has it turned on. Google Play Family Library is under consideration; the current state is on the paywall in the app.

What about the kids' apps?

Kids' apps never require a sign-in. On iPhone the Pass works without an account; on Android the Google account link sits behind a parental gate and is an optional step.

What if I return the Pass?

Refunds are handled by the App Store or Google Play under their rules. After a refund the Pass disappears from every app at the same time.

Is $49 really fixed?

Yes. The value of the unlocks above grows with every app, the price of the Pass does not. Whoever buys earlier gets more for the same money.

The purchase always goes through the App Store or Google Play. The entitlement is shared between the apps through my server; there are no licence keys outside the stores.
