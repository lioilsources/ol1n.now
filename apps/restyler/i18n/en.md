tagline: A selfie, a new beard, glasses or haircut, and then a whole photo booth of you in different styles
---
Restyler is a photo booth in your phone. Take a selfie, try on a new beard,
glasses or haircut, and the app then runs the new look through several styles:
Vermeer, Van Gogh, anime, comics, film noir. The result is a grid or a strip
like the ones from a train station photo booth, except instead of four identical
grimaces you're in a different world in every frame and still recognisably you.

### Step by step

The wizard goes in order **beard → glasses → hair → styles**. Every step can be
skipped with the **Keep** button, so you can just recolour your hair or send
the original selfie straight to the styles.

- **Beard:** 21 cuts from stubble through a full beard to a garibaldi, or a
  clean shave.
- **Glasses:** 26 frames, prescription and sunglasses, with a choice of finish.
- **Hair:** 35 hairstyles for women and men and 16 colours. You can also keep
  your hairstyle and change only the colour.
- **Styles:** 4, 6 or 8 frames from 90 styles across eras, painters and media.
  Each style comes as a painting or as a photo. If you don't want to choose,
  take a ready-made mix: Gallery, Pop & Comics, Journey Through the Ages, Asia,
  Czech Trail.

Every option has a preview, so you see what you're picking before anything
gets rendered.

### Photo booth

The finished frames are laid out in a grid or in a strip with captions. The
strip can be saved to Photos or shared right away. Every project stays on your
phone and opens even without internet.

### What runs on the phone

Almost everything. Face, hair, eye and beard detection is done by a model right
on the phone, and so are the masks, the hair colour estimate, the prompts and
putting the strip together. Only one photo with the masks goes to the server,
and the finished images come back. Rendering runs on our own GPU server, not in
someone else's cloud. The app has no accounts, ads or payments.

Restyler is still in closed testing: on iPhone via TestFlight, on Android via
Firebase.
