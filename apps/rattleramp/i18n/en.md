tagline: A tablet half-pipe for the U Chřestýše bar — draw tricks with your finger on a 3×3 grid and let go at the right moment
---
RattleRamp is a game for the tablet that hangs on the bar at U Chřestýše, next
to the skatepark. A guest waiting for a beer or food taps the screen and rides.
A run lasts from half a minute to two minutes, nothing needs explaining and
nothing needs setting up. Then the tablet goes back to playing runs to itself
to lure the next player.

### How to ride

The skater rides the half-pipe on his own, wall to wall. You do just one thing:
at every lip of the ramp, draw a trick with your finger on the 3×3 grid in the
bottom right, much like drawing a phone unlock pattern. What matters is **the
moment you let go**. A ring closes in on the lip, and the instant it lands, the
skater takes off. Letting go exactly on time is PERFECT, a little off is GOOD,
too late is LATE. Miss it, get the shape wrong or draw a longer trick than the
air time allows, and you bail and the run is over.

Without tricks the skater loses speed, and after a few empty airs he no longer
makes it up to the lip. Landed tricks speed him up, speed lifts the airs, and
higher airs fit longer tricks. But the release window narrows with speed, so
the game turns up the pressure on its own.

### Tricks combine

The grid has a grammar, not a list of shapes. **A circle around the edge** is a
spin (clockwise frontside, counter-clockwise backside, from 180 to 1080). **A
hook through the centre** is a grab: Indy, Melon, Mute, Stalefish, Method,
Japan, Crail, Rocket Air and more, each with its own shape. **A diamond** is a
flip: Kickflip, Heelflip, Varial, 360 Flip, Hardflip, Impossible… Strokes chain
in a single finger motion, each starting where the previous one ended, giving
combos like Kickflip Melon or the named McTwist and Rodeo. Six spins, 14 grabs,
12 flips and 5 named combos add up to thousands of tricks. A test walks through
all of the more than ten thousand combinations of up to three strokes and makes
sure every shape can be read only one way.

### Score

Trick points are multiplied by timing, speed, streak and variety: the same
trick a second time in a run is worth half, a third time a quarter. Every day
one trick is the **trick of the day**, worth double. Beginners get a hint at the
bottom with three shapes to trace. Don't want it? Choose **PRO**: no hints and
1.5× points. At the end of the run a card shows your score, best trick, longest
streak and average timing.

**Trick book** is right on the start screen: every grab, flip, spin and combo
with its drawn shape, points and difficulty.

### Characters and three styles

Before the run you get five seconds to pick a character. Pick nothing and you
ride with the last one. There are eight characters, the first being a
rattlesnake, the bar's mascot, with a rattle on its tail. On top of that, six
outfit colours and six deck graphics. The heads were made with the
FLUX.1-schnell model on our own server; the body and limbs are drawn in code so
spins, flips and grabs can be combined without hand animation for every
combination.

The whole game switches between three styles: **neon** (glowing lines on a dark
background), **comic** (ink outlines, halftone and speed rays) and **pixel**
(the whole scene in big pixels). Switch with the STYL button on the start
screen.

### Status

The core game, the tricks and the characters are done. Still missing is what
makes it a bar game: daily and weekly leaderboards with initials (and a prize
for today's winner, set by the bar), replays of the best runs on the start
screen, a two-player mode at one table, and a locked tablet kiosk mode with an
admin for the staff.

The builds below are test builds (alpha). The game is made for a landscape
tablet; on a phone everything is tiny. Offline, no accounts and no ads.
