---
tagline: A running app that tells you in your earbuds how you're doing against your own form. No account, no ads
---
Běhánek does only what you need when your running shoes are on. It records your run,
shows it on a map and, while you run, tells you briefly in your earbuds how you're doing
against your own form. No feed, no kudos, no badges, no training plans.

### Pacer: you race yourself

From your recent runs of a similar length (up to ten in the last 60 days) Běhánek works
out your typical pace for every kilometer. That's today's goal: just hold it, or go 2 %
faster. Every 30 s, minute, two or five minutes it tells you how far you've gone and how
you're doing, e.g. “three kilometers, pace five ten, five seconds faster”. An
announcement takes a few seconds.

Prefer no voice? Use **tones**: two short mean speed up, one long slow down, and silence
means you're on target. Music in another app only gets quieter during an announcement, it
never stops. The voice speaks 15 languages.

**Ghost** is the second mode. Pick one of your earlier runs that started within 300 m of
where you're standing and race it: “143 m ahead of the ghost”.

Until you have at least three runs, the pacer doesn't make anything up and only announces
time and distance.

### While you run

Big numbers you can read even in sunlight: time, distance, current pace (over the last
~200 m) and average. A 3‑2‑1 countdown gives you time to pocket the phone. You stop only
with a slide (slide to stop), so it never ends by accident in your pocket. Then you choose:
save it, keep running, or discard it.

Auto-pause at traffic lights and a cadence metronome (160–190 steps per minute) can be
switched on in settings. One button switches to a live map of your route. Points are saved
continuously, so if the app crashes, the run is saved on the next launch.

### History and run detail

Runs are grouped by month. For each month you see the distance, the number of runs and
whether your pace is faster than last month. Any month can become a **report** image (km
per week, pace trend, longest run) to send to whoever you like.

The run detail shows the route on a map with kilometer markers, splits (the fastest
kilometer is highlighted), elevation gain, a pace chart and an elevation profile. The map
is outdoor (contours, trails) or satellite. You can trim the start and end if you forgot
to stop.

### Your data is yours

Běhánek has no account, no ads and no third-party analytics. Routes stay on your phone
and your location is never sent anywhere. Only anonymous usage statistics with no
identifiers go to the developer's own server, and you can turn them off in Settings. The app asks for location only “while using the
app”, never “always”.

Every run can be **exported as GPX** and all of them backed up to a ZIP. Export is always
free.

### Import from Strava or Garmin

The only paid thing is **importing** your history: a one-time €0.99, no subscription. It
reads single GPX files as well as a full Strava bulk export (ZIP), so the pacer has your
form to work with from your very first run. Before you pay, you can preview for free what
will be imported.

The app and the voice speak 15 languages, distances in km or miles.
