AlienMatch does everything the opposite of ordinary dating apps. No retouched selfies, no bio, no swiping.

### The alien face

Your profile photo is taken live from the camera only, and the app builds a symmetrical “alien” face out of two identical halves. You choose which one is yours. Only that face goes out into the world; the original never leaves your phone. It can’t be stolen from Instagram or tracked down with a reverse image search.

### Without words

Instead of an age, a generation; instead of text, icons: what you feel like doing (coffee, a walk, wine…) and who you are looking for. The only thing you write is a nickname.

### A radar that reads your mood

Once a day you send a short signal about how you are doing. The radar then shows people as planets on a star map and matches by mood and by where it is heading. Two people who are both feeling low will never be connected. Whoever is feeling up can become a beacon for someone who wants to cheer others on.

### Places without tracking

You can add up to eight places to your profile: your stop, your tram, a café or your faculty. The app never receives your location from GPS. A shared place only shows up when more than one person has it.

### Status

In development. The backend and the first version of the app are done; it is not in the stores yet. No ads and no tracking. Adults only (18+).
