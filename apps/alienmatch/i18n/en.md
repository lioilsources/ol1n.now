tagline: Dating without words, for aliens
---
AlienMatch is a dating app where nobody types. Instead of a photo you get a symmetrical “alien” face, instead of messages hand-written stickers on a shared wall, and instead of swiping a star-map radar.

### A face that isn't your photo

Your profile picture is taken live from the camera only. The app builds a symmetrical version (left + left or right + right half) and only the one you pick is uploaded. The original is never shown or stored.

### Matching by mood

A daily check-in on a scale from planet to supernova decides who shows up on your radar. Two people who are both “low” never connect; someone who is “high” can, with both opting in, cheer up someone having a harder day. The app never shows numbers, only an aura and its trend.

### A wall instead of a chat

After you wave at each other, a wall appears where you stick hand-written stickers or ready-made presets. No text field, no keyboard.

### Privacy

The server never receives your location. Shared places come from a catalogue and a Bluetooth “Flyby” without coordinates. No ads, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.

Built with Flutter, backend in Go and Postgres (repo `AlienMatch`).
