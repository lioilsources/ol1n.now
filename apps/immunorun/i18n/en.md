tagline: A roguelite where you play as the immune system
---
An action roguelite where you control a macrophage and defend the body against pathogens. Room after room, wave after wave — and after each one you choose which door to take next: a fight, an elite enemy, treasure, or a boss.

The upgrades are real immunology. Opsonization marks a target, histamine speeds you up, pyrogen stokes a fever — and fever cuts both ways: it burns the pathogens, but if it gets out of hand, it burns you too.

Death is not the end. Memory cells and vaccination carry over between runs, so you meet the next infection stronger.

The game is in development: the loop, meta-progression, sound and menus are done. Instead of coloured circles, photorealistic cells and bacteria now roam the game: the player is a macrophage, the swarm is made of cocci, the tank is a biofilm and the boss is a staphylococcus that swells into a toxic cluster in its second phase. The upgrade cards have no artwork yet.

The game also includes a book, **Lexikon zdraví**: thirteen chapters for kids aged 10 to 15 about how the body really defends itself. It opens from the menu or right from the game, for example with the “What killed me?” button after you lose. Where the game simplifies for the sake of gameplay, the book says so.

Built with Flutter and Flame (repo `BioDefenseRogue`).
