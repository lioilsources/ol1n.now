---
tagline: Vertical arcade shooter with a roguelike loop
---
You fly alone through an endless corridor of enemies, one sixty-second sector at a time.

Death doesn't wipe your progress. You keep your weapons, credits and score and head back to the first sector to fly further than last time — only the hull resets.

Enemies die according to the weapon that shot them down: the bubble gun splashes them into water, the cannon shatters them into ice, the star cannon sets them ablaze, the laser leaps across them as lightning and the blaster collapses them into purple plasma.

Between sectors you re-arm in the Com Center — front and side weapons, generators, hull and shields, all upgradable across twenty-five levels. Energy is finite, though, so a heavier weapon is always a trade-off.

Eighteen hand-built sectors across six zones, followed by a generator that never runs out and whose difficulty has no ceiling. Two-player co-op over a local network with no accounts or servers, fourteen visual styles, and play by touch, keyboard or gamepad.
