Archion is a strategic chess variant for two — with its own piece movement and victory by controlling all five power fields, bringing down both king and queen, or breaking through with a pawn to the opponent's back rank.

### Capturing is a duel

When you move onto an opponent's piece, the two face off as ships in the arena. You steer your ship with your finger: sideways to dodge, forward to climb, back to descend. A shot only hits a ship at the same altitude. Each piece fires its own element — the pawn kinetic, the knight water, the bishop fire, the rook ice, the queen and king electricity.

Draw a gesture on the 3×3 grid and your ship flies the **maneuver** on its own: a dodge, a loop, a roll, a pass through the arena, or its piece's signature maneuver. Maneuvers cost energy, and four are ready at a time. You earn new ones by winning duels, with credits, or in a pack.

### Play against the computer

Three difficulty levels, either colour. The computer plays the board and flies its own ship in the arena. Duels earn credits you spend in the Comcenter to upgrade units and maneuvers. Ten fleets with their own ship art, music matched to the attacking piece.

### Two players

Hot-seat on one device, over local Wi-Fi, or online via **Supabase Realtime** with opponent disconnect detection. Online games currently capture without a duel.
