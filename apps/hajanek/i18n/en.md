tagline: Bedtime stories for kids — a calm voice, the sound of the sea and a lullaby, no ads and no account
---
Hajánek is an app for the last ten minutes of the day. Play your child a familiar
fairy tale retold as a bedtime meditation: first they settle in and slow their
breathing, then they walk slowly down a forest path with Little Red Riding Hood,
and at the end the characters fall asleep along with them. Underneath, the sea,
the rain or a music box plays softly, and keeps playing for a while after the
story ends.

### Fairy tales where nobody gets scared

Every story has the same calm shape and lasts about ten minutes: an **intro**
(blanket, pillow, breathe in and out), the **way in** to the story, the **story**
itself and the **wind-down**, when the voice slows and softens all the way to a
whisper. The sentences are short, nobody shouts, the child doesn't have to answer
anything and doesn't count anything.

Everything scary is gone. The wolf is old and sleepy, the witch is a kind old
gingerbread granny and there's no oven in the story at all. For every tale we know
exactly what we changed:

- **Little Red Riding Hood** (3+): the wolf doesn't eat or frighten anyone. He's a
  kind old wolf who walks Little Red Riding Hood to Grandma's, and the huntsman just
  waves and goes to bed.
- **Goldenhair** (5+, after Erben): no threats and no death sentence. The tasks are
  calm searches, and the animals help Jiřík with them.
- **Hansel and Gretel** (3+): nobody gets lost and nobody gets locked up. The
  gingerbread granny just laughs when the children nibble her house and gives them
  milk with honey.
- **Sleeping Beauty** (3+): no curse and no spindle. The youngest fairy gives the gift
  of sweet sleep, and the briar hedge tucks the castle in like a quilt.
- **The Three Little Pigs** (3+): the wolf doesn't blow the houses down. His "whoosh"
  is just a yawn, and in the end he warms up by the fire with the pigs.
- **Sweet Porridge** (3+): the porridge doesn't ruin anything. It spreads through the
  village like a big, sweet-smelling pillow, and the little girl calmly says
  "Stop, little pot!".
- **Long, Broad and Sharpsight** (5+, after Erben): no sorcerer and nobody turned to
  stone. The princess just plays hide-and-seek for three evenings.
- **Smolíček and the Golden Deer** (3+): the forest witches are three shy little forest
  fairies who invite Smolíček to a clearing, and in the evening the deer carries him
  home.
- **Budulínek and the Little Fox** (3+): the fox just gives him a ride on her tail to
  her cubs, and Grandpa and Grandma come to fetch him.
- **Snow White** (5+): no queen, no mirror and no apple. Snow White finds the miners'
  little cottage and falls asleep in their little beds.

All ten stories are finished and reviewed. New stories keep arriving in the
catalog, and the app downloads them on its own.

### Jukebox: nature sounds and lullabies

The jukebox plays without a story, so you can leave it on all night. You can mix
up to three layers, each with its own volume. For example, rain on the roof, a quiet
fireplace underneath and a music box on top.

- **Nature sounds:** sea, rain on the roof, a little stream, fireplace, wind in the
  trees, night meadow, pink and brown noise
- **Lullabies:** music box, piano, harp, guitar, little stars (chimes) and dreaming
  (soft pads with no melody), 14 loops in total, all slow, with no drums and no
  singing
- **Presets:** Beach at Dusk, Forest at Night, Rainy Evening, By the Fireplace,
  Gentle Noise. Save your own mix under a name of your choice.

The nature sounds are CC0-licensed recordings. The lullabies were generated on our
own server (ACE-Step, MIT licence). All loops are built into the app and play
without internet.

### The "good night" ritual and the timer

The ritual is a sequence your child will recognise: first only the background plays
for a minute, then the story begins (the background gets quieter), after the story
it lingers for another twenty minutes and finally fades away slowly. You can set
both lengths.

The timer turns the sound off after 5 to 60 minutes or **when the story ends**.
Nothing cuts off abruptly: during the last minute the volume slowly fades out. The
**voice ↔ background** slider lets you balance how much of the storytelling you hear
and how much of the sea.

### The phone's voice, or yours

The story is read by the **phone's voice** in the app's language. It works right away
and without internet. If your phone doesn't have one, the app tells you where to
install it (on iPhone, ideally in Enhanced quality).

But the story can also be read in a **parent's voice**. In the app you read about
thirty seconds of text aloud, the beginning of Little Red Riding Hood, which scrolls
across the screen like song lyrics. Overnight, our server turns the recording into
your voice, and the app downloads the stories read sentence by sentence. Sentences
that haven't downloaded yet are read by the phone's voice for now. You can have
more than one voice, say Mum and Dad. You can delete the recording and the voice at
any time, from the phone and from the server. The parent's voice is still a trial
feature, Czech only, available to testers. In the downloadable version and in the
stores it's switched off, and the app never asks for the microphone.

### Nine languages

The app, all the stories and the phone's voice are available in Czech, English,
German, Spanish, French, Brazilian Portuguese, Italian, Polish and Slovak. The
stories aren't a literal translation but a retelling with the same calm shape. The
language follows your phone, and you can change it in Settings.

### On TV

On Android TV and Google TV, Hajánek has its own remote-friendly interface with the
stories, background sounds and settings. It's just as dark as on the phone.

### A dark room

The whole app is dark, in a muted warm colour with no white areas, so it won't
dazzle. The buttons are big enough to hit even in the dark. After a few seconds the
player screen dims on its own, and the only thing moving on it is a slowly breathing
circle (which you can turn off too). The lock screen shows the story title, the part
being read right now, and pause.

### No ads, no account

Hajánek has no ads, no sign-up and no analytics. The stories and sounds live on your
phone and play offline. The app only uses the network to download new stories and
to upload your voice, if you want that.

The app is for children aged roughly three to eight.
