MirrorBooth creates a symmetrical mirror of your face from the selfie camera in real time.
A playful toy that shows what a perfectly symmetrical face would look like.

### Mirror

One tap switches which half of your face gets mirrored. Drag to rotate the image
and the mirror axis to create kaleidoscopic faces. Works with both the front
and back camera. Take photos with the on-screen shutter or the volume button.

### Filters

Twenty-four filters run live on the camera feed, in four collections:

- **Pretty** (free) — Glow, Slim, Doll, Chibi, Lips
- **Ugly** (free) — Nose, Alien, Melt, Ears, Neck
- **Art** — Pencil, Comic, Glitch, Neon, Heat, Oil, CRT, Pop
- **Fantasy** — Vampire, Zombie, Ghost, Demon, Cyborg, Frost

Previewing every filter is free. Art and Fantasy are one-time purchases that
unlock saving photos and videos with those filters. Restore purchases brings them
back on a new device too. No subscription.

### Video with voice effects

Clips up to 60 seconds with sound. You can add an effect to your voice: 8-bit, Slow,
Chipmunk, Echo, Underwater, Robot or Reverse. The result is saved to your gallery.

### Privacy

The image is processed only on your phone. No account, ads or analytics. Photos
and videos are never sent anywhere.

Built with Flutter, filters are GLSL shaders.
