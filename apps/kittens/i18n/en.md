A card game for two to five players where what counts isn't what's in your hand but how fast you throw it down. Every turn is timed, you play with three lives, and whoever hesitates loses a turn — or a life.

There's a bomb in the deck. Once it turns up, it starts to smoulder and three turns later it blows up on whoever's turn it is. You can defuse it, launch it at someone else, or raise a shield in time.

Eight action cards change the rules mid-game: **Nope!** cancels the last card, **Steal** takes the turn from a player who hesitates, **Flip** switches matching from colour to number and **Blind** hides the card you're playing on. Whoever loses all their lives stays at the table as a ghost and may throw a Nope! once per round.

The game is in development: the core rules, bots and a single-phone table are done; next up is local multiplayer between phones, with no accounts and no servers.

Built in Flutter on a custom pure-Dart engine (repo `Kittens`).
