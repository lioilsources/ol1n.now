Papánek is a cookbook for people who cook rarely or not at all. Type in what you have at home, and the app shows you what you can cook right now, what's missing one thing and what you'll need to shop for. A recipe then walks you through step by step, with a timer on every step where you have to wait.

### What can I cook

The main screen sorts recipes by how much you're missing: **Ready to cook**, **Missing 1 thing**, **Missing 2–3 things**. Salt, pepper, oil and similar basics are taken for granted, so the app won't send you to the shop for them. Add missing ingredients to the shopping list with one tap.

### Recipes written for beginners

Every step does one thing and tells you how to know it's done: colour, smell, texture. With meat and eggs you always know when they're cooked through. Change the servings with the plus and minus buttons and the amounts adjust themselves.

We write the recipes from scratch and never copy them from anywhere. Each one has been checked for language and method: whether the amounts, times and order of steps add up.

### Cooking mode

Large text, one step per screen, a **Next** button and a timer that vibrates and beeps. When you're done cooking, the app offers to remove the used ingredients from your pantry; it never deducts anything without your confirmation.

### Pantry and shopping

Add ingredients by typing, with suggestions that understand Czech with or without diacritics ("kure" finds "kuře"). The pantry is split into categories. Tick items off on the shopping list, and the **Bought** button moves everything into the pantry.

### Content

50 recipes are free, across cuisines: Czech, Italian, Vietnamese, Asian, Mexican and Mediterranean, quick meals and family dishes. More cuisine packs with about 1,500 recipes in total will come as a one-time purchase; everything will be on your phone and the purchase only unlocks it.

### Offline, no account

Papánek needs no internet and no sign-up, and has no ads or analytics. The recipes and pictures are right inside the app. The app is in Czech and English.

Still in development: the first test version 0.1 is on TestFlight for iPhone and available as an APK for Android in the **Downloads** section.
