---
tagline: Picture flashcards for vocabulary in 17 languages, works offline
---
Lexify teaches vocabulary with picture flashcards. Each card pairs a word
with a picture, a short definition and pronunciation from your device's
voice. You swipe through the cards and mark what you know and what you
don't; spaced repetition then brings back the words you struggle with more
often, and the ones you already know less often.

### Language pair

Pick a language you know and a language you're learning. Every deck works in
all **17 languages**: Arabic, Chinese, Czech, English, French, German, Greek,
Hebrew, Hindi, Indonesian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish,
Turkish and Vietnamese. You can add the same deck in another pair at no
extra cost.

### Fifteen decks of fifty cards

Pets, colours, numbers and **world flags** are free. Flags shows the fifty
most populous countries: the flag, the country, its capital and population.
Birds, farm animals, insects, reptiles and amphibians, sea animals, wild
animals, body parts, emotions, fruit, vegetables and weather are one-time
purchases. A purchased deck unlocks for every language pair and style, with
no subscription.

Every illustrated deck comes in two picture styles: photos and one drawn
style (brush and ink, pastel or watercolour). You can switch at any time.

### Offline, no account, no ads

A deck's pictures download once, then everything runs on your device. No
account, no ads, no third-party analytics, and your progress never leaves your
phone. On iOS, macOS and desktop the app sends anonymous usage statistics with
no identifiers to the developer's own server, and you can turn them off in
Settings; on Android it sends nothing.
You can report a wrong translation or picture straight from the card.

Built with Flutter (repo `DuolingoCards`). Runs on iOS, Android, macOS,
Windows and Linux.
