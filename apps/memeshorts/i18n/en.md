A command-line tool that turns a meme template into a six- to fifteen-second vertical short. The original meme serves only as a blueprint for the structure of the joke — it never makes it into the output. Your own characters play the parts instead.

A prompt or a seed can't keep a character consistent, so the images aren't generated for each short. They're made once, as an approved *character × emotion* library: Kiro, Mei, Taro and Yuki, each in twelve expressions from smugness to exhaustion.

The short then runs through the pipeline `classify → storyboard → generate → animate → compose → qa`. Every step is stored separately and keyed by a hash of its options, so a rerun is free and a text change recomputes only what it has to. The joke is written by an LLM or you type it in yourself; subtitles are rendered directly in Go.

The output is 1080×1920, H.264, 30 fps, loudness −14 LUFS, with an “AI generated” label both in the picture and in the metadata.

For now it runs only locally and has no released version, so there's nothing to download here.
Written in Go; images are generated by FLUX.1-schnell on my own machine.
