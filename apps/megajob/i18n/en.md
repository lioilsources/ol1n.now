MegaJob is a tool for renovating flats and houses. It breaks a job into steps,
staffs them with tradespeople, keeps the schedule and shows what is really
happening on site — even when you are a hundred kilometres away.

This page describes what we are building. The pictures are draft screens, not
screenshots of a finished app, and they are in Czech. Names and addresses in
them are made up.

### Who it is for

- **Coordinator** — an architect, designer, site manager or property manager
  who today runs renovations from a phone and group chats. Handles more jobs
  at once.
- **Tradesperson** — one calendar for everyone they work for. Fewer calls,
  less driving, proof of the work done.
- **Customer** — sees who is on site, what got done and what comes next. No
  need to take a day off to watch the flat.

### A job as a flow

A renovation is a chain of steps that build on each other: inspections,
demolition, plumbing and wiring, masonry, tiling, fit-out, handover. Between
them sit drying pauses, material deliveries and checks before things get
covered up. MegaJob holds that flow together. A step can be added at any time,
even to a job already under way, and every change is a new version of the
quote that the customer approves.

### Work is offered, not assigned

Tradespeople keep their own price list and their own calendar. They accept an
offer, decline it or propose another date. Declining costs nothing. Until the
date is locked, they may release accepted work if something better comes
along; the slot is offered to the next person and the customer knows about
the change.

### Site diary and remote oversight

On arrival the app checks that the tradesperson is on site. Coordinates are
not stored and nothing is collected outside the shift. Before and after photos
come only from the app's camera. The customer sees “on site from 7:42 to
15:10” and the day's diary, not a dot on a map.

### Payments in plain sight

No money flows through MegaJob. The customer pays the tradesperson directly
with a QR code; MegaJob matches the payment and shows what is paid and what is
overdue.

### Ratings from facts

Tradespeople are rated on arrivals, the diary, complaints and how the next
trade received their work. Customers are rated on their payments. Neither
ratings nor the order of tradespeople can be bought.

### Free and paid

The basics will be free. Visibility of a job, speed and extras will be paid,
and anything paid will always be marked.

### Status

In preparation. The plan and the draft screens are done. The first version
will be a tool for coordinators: the job, shifts, the diary and remote
oversight. Sharing spare capacity between coordinators, payments in plain
sight and jobs without a coordinator come later.
