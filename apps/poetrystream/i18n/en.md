tagline: A meditative stream of Czech poetry

StreamPoetry turns the poetry of contemporary Czech poet Simon Clinae
into a meditative experience. Lines light up on a dark, slowly breathing
background, linger for a moment and fade away again. Every stanza gets its
own typeface, colour and size. On the App Store and Google Play the app is
called **Poesie Simona Clinae**.

### Three modes

- **Stream**: stanzas from all the poems blend at random and change on their own
- **Poem**: one poem, stanza by stanza, at a calm pace
- **Reading**: the whole poem at once; swipe from poem to poem, round and round

The **ŽIVĚ / PAUZA** (live / pause) button stops the stream, and **BÁSNĚ**
(poems) lets you pick a specific poem from the list.

### Collections

The collections Pravdu má jen jeden (13 poems) and Ježíš hraje na sitár
(5 poems) are free. A one-time purchase adds Kožní ambulance (18 poems)
and Smrtelně vážně (12 poems). No subscription, and purchases can be restored
on a new device.

### Typography

Twelve typefaces, ten colour palettes with softly glowing text, six sizes
and the occasional italic. The animated, grainy background is like paper
in the dark.

### Privacy

No ads, no accounts, no analytics. Settings stay on your device.

The poems speak openly about the body, sexuality, illness, death and faith,
and at times use coarse language. Built with Flutter, runs on desktop and
mobile. Both the poems and the interface are in Czech.
