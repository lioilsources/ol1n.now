---
tagline: Your kid taps “I'm here” at their after-school club and you know. No location tracking
---
Automatka is for parents whose kids already get to their after-school clubs on
their own. At the venue, the kid taps one big **I'm here** button, the server
checks they really are at the sports hall or the youth centre, and the parent
gets a notification. If the kid hasn't checked in by the time the club starts,
a notification comes too. That's it: no map and no background tracking.

### Location only on a button tap

Coordinates leave the phone only at the moment the kid taps the button. The
server compares them with the club's location, stores only “was / wasn't
there” and throws the coordinates away. So the parent never sees which way the
kid went, only that they arrived, left and are home.

### For kids

- **I'm here**, then **Leaving** and **I'm home**. Big buttons, no menu.
- **SOS** has to be held for two seconds so it doesn't go off in a pocket.
- With no signal, the tap is saved and sent as soon as the phone reconnects.
- If the phone can't get a location, checking in still works. The parent then
  sees that the arrival wasn't verified by location.
- No account and no password. The kid pairs with a six-digit code or a QR code
  from the parent, and the parent can unpair the device at any time.

### For parents

The day overview shows each kid and their clubs: waiting, there, left. The
“no check-in” alert disappears as soon as the kid checks in or the parent
acknowledges it. The other parent or grandma sees the same. The server can
export a family or delete it entirely, accounts included; there's no button for
that in the app yet.

### Status

Automatka is in Phase 0: checking that pairing, push, offline mode and GPS
inside sports halls work on real phones and watches. The backend and the
screens for kids and parents are in place and have passed tests on a local
server. Managing places and clubs in the app, invites for the other parent and
the watch app come after that. Nothing to download yet.
