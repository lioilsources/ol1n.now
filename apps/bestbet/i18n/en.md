tagline: A hockey model that shows the fair probability of a game next to the odds
---
BestBet calculates its own probabilities for hockey games: who wins in regulation, how many goals are scored, how the first period ends. Next to them it shows the odds from bookmakers licensed in Czechia and the difference between the two. Whether and how much to bet is up to you.

### Team wave

Every team has its own "wave": a form estimate that shifts after each game based on the result and the strength of the opponent. The app shows its level, its direction and how confident the estimate is. The wave describes form, it doesn't promise a result.

### Honest with numbers

The model is tested on seasons it never saw while it was being built, and its accuracy is always shown next to the market's. The app shows a green-badge recommendation only for markets where the model has beaten the closing odds over the long run. Until it proves that, nothing turns green.

It also has a bet journal that tells you how you're doing against the closing odds.

### Status

In development, the app doesn't exist yet. What's done: collecting games, goalies and odds from Sweden's HockeyAllsvenskan and the NHL, and a model that calculates probabilities from them.

The first test against real odds didn't work out: in both leagues the model is worse than the market. The next step is a model that starts from the market odds and adjusts them for goalies and form. Until it passes, the app won't be built.
