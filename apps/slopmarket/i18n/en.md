SlopMarket is a catalogue of print-ready motifs. Every image was made on our own GPU
server and is ready to end up on a T-shirt, mug, poster, canvas, cushion,
phone case or as a full-wall mural. The name is a confession: this is AI
work, and every image is labelled as such.

### What you'll find in the catalogue

- **T-shirt prints:** standalone graphics on a clean background, ready
  for DTG and DTF printing.
- **Patterns and wallpapers:** seamless repeating patterns, from botanical leaves to
  art deco and kids' rooms.
- **Posters and pictures:** animals, dinosaurs, machines, landscapes, dogs and cats
  in watercolour, linocut, oil painting, pixel art or retro poster style.
- **Plushies and portraits:** plush toys and imagined faces in the styles of Vermeer,
  Van Gogh, anime or film noir. The people in the portraits do not exist.

### What it's good for

Each motif has a table showing which products it suits and up to what size:
✅ prints as is, ⬆ after upscaling, ⚠ with cropping, ✖ not suitable. It is calculated
from the original's dimensions and aspect ratio, so you see right away whether the motif
is enough for a 50×70 poster or just a mug.

### A chat that changes the selection

The chat is at the top, the gallery with filters below. Type “fox in watercolour on a mug”
and the gallery switches to watercolour foxes that suit a mug. It works
even without a language model: words like “portrait”, “watercolour” or “for a T-shirt”
are recognised and turned into filters, the rest goes to full-text search.

### Where to get a motif printed

You can have a motif printed at ordinary photo services and print shops in Czechia
and Slovakia. Indicative prices per piece including VAT, excluding shipping
(checked on 17 November 2026, promotions change):

- **T-shirt print:** 119–450 Kč, SK from 16,90 € — [Vytvořsipotisk](https://vytvorsipotisk.cz/potisk-tricek/), [MyShirt](https://www.myshirt.cz/), [Navrhnisi.sk](https://navrhnisi.sk/)
- **Mug:** 69–300 Kč, SK from 10,90 € — [Fotodárky](https://www.fotodarky.cz/), [CEWE](https://www.cewe.cz/)
- **Poster A3 / A2:** from 99 Kč / from 299 Kč — [CEWE](https://www.cewe.cz/), [Fotodárky](https://www.fotodarky.cz/)
- **Stretched canvas 40×60:** around 709 Kč — [CEWE](https://www.cewe.cz/), [Fotodárky](https://www.fotodarky.cz/)
- **Wall mural:** 490–850 Kč/m² — [Dantek](https://dantek.cz/fototapeta), [Fotožaluzie](https://www.fotozaluzie.cz/fototapeta-z-vlastni-fotografie/), [Bricoflor](https://www.bricoflor.cz/fototapety/vlastni-motiv.html)
- **Cushion:** 159–610 Kč — [Fotodárky](https://www.fotodarky.cz/), [CEWE](https://www.cewe.cz/)
- **Phone case:** 500–800 Kč — [CEWE](https://www.cewe.cz/), [CEWE.sk](https://www.cewe.sk/)
- **Mouse pad:** 129–230 Kč — [Fotodárky](https://www.fotodarky.cz/), [Navrhnisi.sk](https://navrhnisi.sk/)
- **Puzzle:** 249–500 Kč — [CEWE](https://www.cewe.cz/), [Navrhnisi.sk](https://navrhnisi.sk/)

Shipping costs roughly 79–249 Kč in Czechia and around 3,60–3,90 € in Slovakia.
A few tips: plan for 150–300 dpi at the final size (an A3 poster needs about
3500×4960 px); 100–150 dpi is enough for wallpaper. Colours come out most faithfully on a white
T-shirt; for dark ones, use a motif with a transparent background. For wallpaper, measure the wall
in several places and add 5 cm for trimming.
