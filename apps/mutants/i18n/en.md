A cooperative card game for kids. All players throw cards with animal parts into the cauldron at the same time and build one shared creature from them. Nobody loses.

A mutant has six slots: head, body, front and back limbs, tail and something extra. A T. rex head on a turtle shell with crab claws, a peacock tail and bat wings is a perfectly valid result. Every part carries an element and an origin — fire, ice, shadow, dino, insect, robot — and whatever comes together decides the creature's name, power and rarity.

A mutation overwrites a slot that's already taken: two heads, six arms, eyes everywhere. The finished mutant hatches and goes into the bestiary.

One to five players; solo, a “buddy” bot lends a hand. The game is in development — the rules, the bots and a table prototype with hatching and the bestiary are done, and part artwork is being added right now.

Built in Flutter on a custom engine in pure Dart (repo `Mutants`).
