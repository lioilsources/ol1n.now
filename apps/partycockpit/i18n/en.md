tagline: A party show on the big screen — guests send photos, messages and dances from their phones, you control what's shown
---
PartyCockpit turns the TV at your party into a live show. Guests scan the QR code on the screen and send selfies in different styles, messages for the wall, little dances and live camera feeds from their phones. You have the **Cockpit** on a tablet: you see who sent what, approve it and switch what's on the screen right now. No app on the phone, a browser is enough.

### Nothing goes on screen without approval

Every photo, message and dance waits until you let it through. The original never makes it to the screen, only the photo in its new style. The **PANIC** button instantly switches the screen back to a safe slideshow and turns off the live cameras and the microphone.

### Scenes

- **Guest gallery**: selfies as Vermeer, anime or comic, with a live portrait that blinks and smiles
- **Message wall**: every message handwritten, in fonts from HandWrittenStickers; the guest picks the font on their phone
- **Dance floor**: a guest picks a dance and a few minutes later they're dancing on the screen
- **Live**: up to nine phones as cameras in a grid on the screen
- **Subtitles**: say something into the microphone and the transcript appears on the screen, translation included
- **Shader break** and **StyleMap**: an image that breathes to the rhythm of the music
- **Party episode**: a comic made from tonight's photos, with the guests' messages in the speech bubbles
- **Recap** to finish: everything from the evening plus a QR code guests can use to download the photos

### A game for the break

A draw picks the players and they play **Kruh**: each one defends their own arc with a paddle they drag with a finger on their phone. The ball speeds up to the rhythm of the music, and the last one with a life left wins.

### On the beat

Every scene moves with the tempo: from DJfy, which plays the music and takes song requests from guests, from the microphone by the screen, or from tapping the beat in the Cockpit.

### Party box

PartyCockpit runs on a single computer on the party network: a laptop or mini PC by the TV, or a Raspberry Pi 5. Download the package for Windows or Linux, run it and open the screen in a browser. Phones connect over Wi-Fi, nothing gets installed. Photos and messages stay on that computer and delete themselves a week after the party ends. Styles and dances need a GPU server; without one, it only imitates them.

Still in development: tried out on test data, the first real party is yet to come.
