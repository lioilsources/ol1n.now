---
tagline: Arcade biplane dogfights for a group in one room — over WiFi or on one computer
---
DoodlebugsRevival is an arcade flying shooter for several players sitting in the same room. Everyone flies a biplane that always moves forward and only turns. A hit costs shield or hull, and a downed plane reappears behind a cloud. Up top is space, where the engine cuts out and the plane falls.

The game is built in Unity 6 and runs on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iOS.

### A party game

Devices on the same WiFi find each other on their own, with no account, server or setup. Phones without shared WiFi connect directly over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct (iOS Multipeer, Android Nearby), up to eight players. On one computer, four can play at once on keyboard and gamepads.

Until a second player joins, a bot circles the arena showing off loops, stall turns and short bursts. It won't start a battle on its own; the game is built for at least two players.

### Rounds and the hangar

A round ends after three kills or three minutes. A match is first to three rounds, and the winner gets the podium. Between rounds there's the hangar: you pick a weapon from the offer, spend points from the run on shield, hull, fire rate or engine, and hit READY. There are eight weapons: machine gun, twin machine gun, flak, heavy flak, aerial bomb, sniper gun, rocket and a mine that hides in the clouds.

Downed planes drop balloons with health, shield, repair, stronger fire or a better weapon.

### Planes and arenas

There are 28 plane shapes to choose from, from a triplane and a seaplane to a bathtub, a paper airplane, a dragon and an airship, plus 50 camouflages. The shape decides what the shots are made of: the dragon breathes fire, the unicorn shoots lightning, spaceships fire plasma. The hitbox is the same for every shape, so none is harder to hit.

The arenas are Metropolis, MistyPeaks, DuneSea, Volcano and Orbit. The foreground terrain can be shot apart piece by piece, and a bomb blasts a crater into it. The host picks which arenas the run cycles through. Billboards in the arenas advertise only made-up brands.

### Controls

On a computer, arrow keys and space, or a gamepad (Xbox, DualShock). On a phone, a virtual stick and fire triggers; tilt steering is optional in the settings.

No ads, no in-app purchases, no data collected. Desktop and Android are available to download; iOS is in TestFlight only for now.
