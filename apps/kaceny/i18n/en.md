Kačeny is played in portrait with one thumb. Ducks fly from the top of the
screen to the bottom and you shoot them down by tapping. A round lasts 90
seconds, the magazine holds eight rounds, and you reload by swiping down or
tapping the ammo.

### Gameplay

Ducks come in four sizes. Small, distant ones are fast and score more, big
close ones are easy. A clean body hit counts separately, and a streak of hits
without a miss raises the multiplier. Every now and then the head of a big duck
peeks in from the edge of the screen, and it's worth more. The shot fires the
moment you touch the screen, not when you lift your finger, and the game takes
into account where the duck was at the instant of your tap. If you'd rather not
reload by hand, turn on auto-reload.

Scores go to a local leaderboard (top 20 with initials) and to Game Center.
The game has no ads, needs no account and works offline. It's available in
eight languages: Czech, English, German, Polish, Slovak, Spanish, French and
Italian.

### Skins

The game has 113 skins in eleven packs: retro pixel art, watercolor, ukiyo-e,
stained glass, cyberpunk, Christmas, Czech blueprint, gold and more. Each skin
changes both the duck and the background in a matching style. Ducks and
backgrounds were generated on my own GPU server (Flux), the ducks were cut out
by the RMBG model, and the wing flapping was made from short videos with the
Wan 2.2 model.

The base pack with 13 skins is free. Ten themed packs (Retro arcade, Art
gallery, Cute, Materials, Neon & night, Holidays & seasons, World folk art,
Fantasy & sci-fi, Nature & realism, Luxury) are sold individually as one-time
in-app purchases, or all at once. Nothing you can buy gives you an advantage in
the game.

For now this is a test build. An APK is available for Android; the game isn't
out on the App Store or Google Play yet.
