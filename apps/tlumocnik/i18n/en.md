---
tagline: A phone on the table between two people — each speaks their own language and the app says the translation aloud to the other
---
Tlumočník is for a conversation at one table: at a government office, at the repair shop, at the doctor's, with a new colleague. You put the phone in the middle, you speak Czech, the other person speaks, say, Ukrainian. The app works out on its own who is speaking, translates it and says it out loud to the other person. Nobody taps anything and you don't pass the phone back and forth.

### A screen split in half

The bottom half is yours, the top half is turned upside down for the person across from you. Each of you reads in your own language and nobody has to read anything upside down. The large text is always what that person understands: for your sentences, what you said; for the other person's, their translation. The small text above it is the other version, so you can see exactly what was said. A coloured bar along the edge shows whose side is speaking.

The translated text appears before the app reads it out. If you don't want the phone to talk, switch to **Text only** and the conversation carries on like subtitles.

### Who's speaking

The app decides by voice and by language. After a few sentences it remembers what each person sounds like, and until then it helps that each of you speaks a different language. When it isn't sure, it doesn't guess: it shows the sentence on both halves without a translation and with a **That was me** button. Whoever said it taps, and the app translates it in the right direction. You can also long-press your half to set the speaker yourself.

While the phone is talking, the microphone doesn't listen, so the app doesn't translate itself.

### Three modes

- **Auto:** one person speaks at a time and the app works out by itself when each person started and finished.
- **Push to talk:** keep your finger on your half for as long as you speak. Handy in noisy places where the automatic mode isn't enough.
- **Live:** a running transcript appears while you speak.

### 13 languages

Czech, English, German, Ukrainian, Vietnamese, Polish, Slovak, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese and Chinese. You pick the language pair before you start; the most recently used ones are at the top of the list.

### History on your phone

Every conversation is saved to history with a time for each sentence. You can read it again, select text and share the whole thing, for example by email as meeting notes. History stays only on your phone.

### Your own voice (experimental)

The translation can also sound like you. You record thirty seconds of continuous reading, and the app then says your sentences in the foreign language in a voice that resembles yours. A voice is biometric data, so recording only works with the consent box ticked, you may record only yourself, and deleting a voice physically removes the recording from the server. You can have at most five voices. Your own voice is for now only available to testers with an invite code.

### Access

The app has no account: on first launch the phone registers itself as a device. Without a code it gets basic access with limited translation time per day. An invite code from the app's author unlocks tester access with longer translation time and your own voice. When today's minutes run out, the app tells you, and tomorrow it translates again.

### Privacy

Speech recognition, translation and the voice all run on our own GPU server, not in someone else's cloud. The server doesn't store audio or conversation texts and doesn't write them to logs. The app has no account, ads or analytics. Because of the server, it needs an internet connection.

Tlumočník is still in closed testing: on iPhone via TestFlight, on Android via Firebase.
