---
tagline: Stickers made from your own handwriting
---
Type a text and the app assembles it character by character from handwritten
letters — then saves it as a transparent PNG you can send as a sticker.

The first set is real children's handwriting: Laurinka filled in an A4
template, it was photographed, and a tool cut 160 characters out of it,
including the full range of Czech diacritics. Anyone can print the same
template and fill it in. The second scanned set, the cursive Jessie 1, joins
the letters within a word with a single pen stroke, like real joined-up writing.

On top of that come more than eighty other sets in three categories —
handwriting, calligraphy and typography — plus kids' and material sets: bubble,
plasticine, gingerbread, neon. You switch sets right in the editor, and the text
is set on a baseline with proportional spacing, so it doesn't look cut and pasted.

### Presets and emoji

A preset is a set together with a style: spacing, line height, baseline wobble,
letter size and rotation. Built in are Neat, Casual, Quick Note and Chaos; more
come with the packs (Love Letter, Cake Lettering, Kid's Note, Neon Sign…). You
can save your own settings as a new preset. The app remembers where you left
off the next time you open it.

You can add one of 41 hand-drawn emoji stickers to a message, gently swaying.
With one attached, the sticker is saved as an animated GIF that messengers can
send too.

### Free, with extra packs

The editor, built-in presets, PNG and GIF export, emoji and joined letters are
free. What will cost money is extra content — themed font packs with their own
presets (Handwriting, Calligraphy, Posters & Lettering, For Kids, Materials) —
and saving your own presets. You can try a locked set in the editor; payment
only comes up when you save. The packs will be sold on the App Store and Google
Play, where the app isn't available yet. In the Windows and Linux version
everything is unlocked.

Two parts: a Go tool that turns a template scan or a font into a character set,
and a Flutter app that composes text from it (repo `HandWrittenStickers`).
